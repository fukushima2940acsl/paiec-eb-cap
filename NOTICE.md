AI assistance during development:

- **Claude:** assistance with initial data preparation, prediction models, and experiment code.
- **Codex:** assistance with local evaluation corrections, eb_cap and ablation implementations, validation, submission packaging, reproducibility tooling, and documentation.
- **Author:** execution of experiments, interpretation and selection of methods, Codabench submissions, and review of release contents.

AI-assisted code is checked through tests and experiments. Synthetic local tests and real-data reproduction on the execution server are documented separately. The competition technical report should disclose the assistance actually used, as requested by the organizers.
